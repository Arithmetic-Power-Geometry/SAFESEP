# SAFESEP-II V4 — static noninterference certificate

This historical stage records a sufficient static certificate considered before the evidence-precedence formulation in the published paper.

A selected separator family is safe under the stronger pairwise noninterference condition when every selected probe is initially executable, the family separates every decision-incompatible pair, and no selected probe revokes a token required by another selected probe.

The complete three-world/two-probe deterministic binary-observation battery contains 5,184 ordered systems under the stated requirement/revocation slice. The published SAFESEP-II paper reports the less restrictive acyclic evidence-precedence certificate as Theorem 1 and retains 5,184 exhaustive precedence cases in its validation table.
