# 2026 adjacent-work addendum

A final September 2026 search was run for the exact phrases "Branch-Relative
Authority Closure", "safe separability" with autonomous authorization, and
closely related decision-critical authorization/evidence-acquisition terms.

No obvious exact prior use of the BRAC name or the full SAFESEP formulation was
identified in the reviewed search results. This negative search result is not a
novelty guarantee.

A relevant 2026 neighboring line is CDEG (Dai et al., 2026), which studies
decision-critical evidence acquisition in long-horizon diagnostic agents.
CDEG reinforces that sequential acquisition of decision-relevant evidence is an
active research topic. Its stated problem, however, concerns evidence that
drives diagnostic decisions; the SAFESEP theorem studied here imposes a
different constraint: an evidence probe must itself be legitimate across all
authorization worlds remaining compatible with the current branch.

The manuscript should therefore avoid claiming novelty for "decision-critical
evidence acquisition" in general. The claimed distinction should remain the
authorization-sensitive admissibility condition, joint knowledge-authority
semantics, and the restricted BRAC characterization.
