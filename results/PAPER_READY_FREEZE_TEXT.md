# SAFESEP V1 publication record

This file is retained as an archival development record. It is not an instruction to modify the published paper.

## Published paper

Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). *Safe Separability for Autonomous Authorization: Branch-Relative Authority Closure Under Decision-Critical Uncertainty* (Version V1). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22828708

## Published contributions

The V1 paper reports:
1. safe-separability semantics for authorization decisions under incomplete knowledge;
2. exact joint knowledge-authority recursion;
3. the cheapest-unresolved-experiment diagnostic for controlled witness families;
4. MCAS closure exactness for a restricted monotone class;
5. counterexamples showing why naive closure fails with informative authority progress, world-dependent grants, and revocation;
6. Branch-Relative Authority Closure (BRAC), which recomputes authority closure after observations relative to the surviving branch; and
7. a reproducibility package with 266 permanent tests, 256 exhaustive four-world systems, 1,250 randomized certified comparisons, and controlled stress up to 10,000 worlds.

## Published theorem boundary

The BRAC theorem in V1 is a sound normalization result under monotone, world-independent authority effects, branch-constant authority-progress probes that leave the compatible-world branch unchanged, authority-dependent legitimacy, and no revocation.

The paper does not publish a stronger iff completeness theorem or polynomial-time characterization. Revocation and world-dependent grants are explicit boundary counterexamples and richer semantics remain outside the V1 theorem.

## Android status

Android-specific temporal-authority work is maintained separately in the Android Temporal Authority Lab. SAFESEP V1 does not use Android experiments as theorem evidence.

For current claims and citation, use the published paper and README.
