# SAFESEP-II revocation frontier — historical V1 record

This file preserves the first revocation-stage result that preceded the published SAFESEP-II V1 paper. It is an archival research record, not a current manuscript instruction.

## Research question

Which non-monotone authority effects can be admitted while retaining sound authorization-sensitive evidence acquisition?

## Early sufficient lemma

At a joint state `(C,A)`, a branch-constant, branchwise legitimate probe with world-independent effects can be moved earlier without disabling later probes through revocation when every token it revokes is absent from the requirements of all probes used later on the relevant continuation.

This requirement-disjoint condition is sufficient but conservative. The published paper replaces this early local view with the stronger theorem chain based on destructive evidence coupling, evidence precedence, branch-conditioned adaptivity, decision obligations, relevant authority, and dynamic behavioral probe types.
